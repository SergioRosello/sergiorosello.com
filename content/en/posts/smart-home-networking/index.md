Before I forget, I want to write down why and how I configured the networking of
my smart home.

# Why

If you read my previous post, you know that I am using HomeAssistant to create
and manage my smart home.

While researching how to start with the HomeAssistant project, I got into self
hosting, and decided I wanted to try and self host HomeAssistant and potentially
other applications too.

The immediate thought after that was: What about security? I had to segment my
network to ensure security was taken care of. I had a public facing service and
security footage on the same network. That is just a recipe for disaster.

Security is just one of those things that you have to take care of from the
beginning of your project, otherwise it becomes harder and harder to implement
along the road.

Also, networking has been my weak point for a long time, so this was the perfect
opportunity to learn and apply the knowledge.
